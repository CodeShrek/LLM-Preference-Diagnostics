# Phase 20: Threshold Relevance Experiment

When the target threshold was lowered to 10 (guaranteeing success at almost any choice), the abstention rate was 84.0% (compared to 83.9% in the main task).

## Findings
In the sensitivity model, the effect of quantitative magnitude (abs_log_Q) on choosing the dominant option was $\beta = 1.273$ ($p = 0.001$).
**CONCLUSION:** The model remains highly sensitive to quantitative magnitude even when the threshold is rendered non-binding. The Q-sensitivity is NOT merely a threshold-crossing artifact; the model actively prefers larger payoffs even when smaller payoffs also suffice to meet the goal.
