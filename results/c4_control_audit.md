# C4 Control Audit (Phase 11)

## Overview
Condition C4 was administered to the same $N=1120$ trial combinations (20 dilemmas × 4 phrasings × 7 ratios × 2 positions) but stripped of the decision-making context, directly asking the model:
*"Which operation produces the greater improvement in score? Respond 'Indifferent' if they produce the same improvement."*

## Results
- **Overall Accuracy:** 1120 / 1120 (100.0%)

### Breakdown
Because accuracy was exactly 100%, there is zero variance across any subgroup:
- **By Q-Ratio:** 1:9 (100%), 1:5 (100%), 1:2 (100%), 1:1 (100%), 2:1 (100%), 5:1 (100%), 9:1 (100%)
- **By Position (Add First vs Subtract First):** 100% vs 100%
- **By Phrasing Variant:** 100% across all 4 variants

## Safe Interpretation
The model showed no detectable failure on the numerical dominance-comprehension probe, making a basic arithmetic/comparison failure an unlikely explanation for the main-task behavior (where it abstained 84% of the time).
